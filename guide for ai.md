EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
HKAP Limited is a Nigerian-incorporated company with over 24 years of experience in industrial recycling and metal scrap management, now expanding into the upstream oil and gas sector through oil rig leasing and drilling operations. The company owns and operates TIKVAH, a heavy-duty swamp barge drilling rig manufactured in 2008, currently moored at Sapele, Delta State.
TIKVAH is a rotary-driven rig with a depth rating of 20,000 ft, capable of drilling both vertical and horizontal wells. HKAP Limited offers oil rig leasing services to reputable operators including the Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPCL), Seplat Energy, Sahara Energy, and other established players in the Nigerian oil and gas industry.
Guided by strong corporate governance, professional ethics and a commitment to client satisfaction, HKAP Limited is supported by an experienced management team and technical workforce dedicated to delivering safe, reliable and sustainable industrial and energy solutions.
ABOUT US
HKAP Limited was incorporated in Nigeria on 6 November 2002, under RC Number 464616, in accordance with the Companies Act 1990. For more than two decades, the company has grown and evolved within the recycling industry, established to manage scrap and waste materials professionally — maximising recycling and minimising waste for disposal, so that clients avoid unnecessary cost and, wherever possible, increase revenue.
The company is led by a management team with deep experience across the recycling and oil and gas industries. HKAP's One-Stop-Centre, built on a Total Waste Management concept, covers all aspects of scrap collection, metal smelting, and waste handling and disposal — giving clients the dual benefit of reduced costs and reduced management time.
This vast experience in metal scrap handling and smelting, including scrap generated by the oil and gas industry, has translated directly into the technical capability now being deployed to operate HKAP's own oil drilling rig.

FLAGSHIP ASSET — TIKVAH DRILLING RIG
TIKVAH (formerly Lonestar 203 / Pride of Africa) is a heavy-duty swamp barge drilling rig built for the riverine, estuarine and shallow-water environments of the Niger Delta. Acquired by HKAP Limited through a transparent, competitive bidding process, the rig carries a clean, unencumbered title, free of liens or legal disputes, and has a proven track record of drilling production wells for major operators in the region.
●Configuration:  Rotary-driven rig, capable of drilling both vertical and horizontal wells
●Depth Rating:  20,000 feet
●Drill Pipe:  5-inch high-tensile drill pipe
●Fluid Circulation:  120 industrial mud/fluid pumps of varying sizes
●Power Plant:  4 x 1,250 HP engines (5,000 HP total)
●Marine Flight Deck:  Fully certified helipad module
●Quartering:  Offshore living quarters for 95 active crew
●Current Location:  Sapele, Delta State, Nigeria
